## Approach

- Sourced prizes for the lucky draw at the Ewha spring festival (Daedongje) in three ways:
  - ₩300K in sponsorship from the Next Challenge Asia Foundation
  - Paper coupons worth about ₩200K sponsored by the festival organizing committee
  - 200+ booth vouchers secured at no cost through partnerships with 6 campus groups
- Ran offline promotion with about 300 A5 posters, 50 A4 posters, 2 banners, and 5 days of costume promotion.
- Ran online promotion through the official Instagram accounts of the festival organizing committee and UNIS.

## Outcome

- 1,392 event entries, 7,555 visitors
- 312 winners, 312 prizes delivered
