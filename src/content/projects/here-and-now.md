## Problem

Most place information is ads or sponsored content and hard to trust, and finding one place doesn't turn into a full date course.

## Approach

- Ran 18 interviews, a survey (n=62), and IDIs (N=11), and summarized the insights as a "cycle of distrust." 78% of interviewees felt stressed when planning a course.
- Designed the IA and screen flows for 3 core features: Explore (trust-based discovery), Archive (course logs), and Connecting (shared logs for couples).
- Validated the market leader's BM through interviews with small business owners, then redesigned it as a voucher BM (20% discount, 30% commission), course-based booking fees, and branded sponsorships.
- Built a 3-stage GTM and sized TAM, SAM, and SOM (₩1.07T / ₩418B / ₩50.4B).

## Outcome

- Presented at the KUSITMS cohort 32 meetup demo day
- Built an MVP with PM, design, FE, and BE teammates
