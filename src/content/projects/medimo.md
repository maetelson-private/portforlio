## Problem

Farms find it inefficient to request care and have no way to track treatment history or vaccinations. Veterinarians manage schedules by phone, and thin treatment records lead them to repeat the same diagnoses.

## Approach

- Defined the problem through interviews and research, then designed the IA and user flows.
- Emergency and routine care requests with real-time vet matching
- Automated treatment records and history for each animal
- Vaccination and medication schedules with reminders
- Designed the full UI/UX and the logo (BI).
