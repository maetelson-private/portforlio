## Problem

Access to arts and culture is uneven across regions, and young artists need places to show their work.

## Approach

- Connected young artists with underserved spaces through a public gallery format.
- Handled exhibition themes, artist recruitment, leaflet and pamphlet orders, exhibition layout and scheduling, artwork management, and MOUs.
- Planned the Aisori Festival with the Paradise Welfare Foundation and supervised it on site.

## Outcome

- Partnered with 10 organizations, including a community welfare center, a fragrance brand, and local shops
